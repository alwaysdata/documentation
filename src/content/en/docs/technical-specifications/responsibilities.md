## What we manage

We are responsible for:

- hardware,
- network,
- operating system,
- base software (e.g. Apache, uWSGI, languages…).

We take care of their [upgrades](/en/docs/technical-specifications/security-upgrades/).

## What you manage

Everything else: your applications, their dependencies, their configuration and their data.
