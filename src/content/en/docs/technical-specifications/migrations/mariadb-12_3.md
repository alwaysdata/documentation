This migration upgrades your databases to MariaDB version 12.3.

It introduces minor changes. To review them, go on the release note(s) involved by your current version (noticeable in
the *Databases > MySQL* section of your account).

- [Upgrade to MariaDB 12.3](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/server-management/install-and-upgrade-mariadb/upgrading/mariadb-community-server-upgrade-paths/upgrading-from-mariadb-11-8-to-mariadb-12-3).
